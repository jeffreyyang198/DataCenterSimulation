# Live dashboard rehearsal

Run `./check_presentation_ready.sh`. Start `./run_presentation_dashboard.sh` and open the URL printed in Terminal. Use a widescreen browser window. The dashboard opens in projector mode.

## 60–90 second sequence

1. **0–10 s · U.S. overview.** Point out the Central scenario, 2030, and the national electricity, CO₂ and direct water cards. These are scenario-based screening estimates.
2. **10–25 s · Virginia.** Click Virginia on the map, or choose it from the state selector. Open **South Fork Shenandoah** (HUC8 02070005) from the watershed selector. Point out where the complete watershed extends beyond the Virginia outline.
3. **25–45 s · Year and scenario.** Set the watershed metric to **Scarcity-weighted water footprint**. Move from 2023 to 2030 and switch from **Central** to **Higher pressure**. Describe the changes as modeled under the selected scenario.
4. **45–60 s · Site.** Click a site marker or choose a site from the dropdown. Point out one annual metric and the nearby population. Use **Clear selected site** to return to the full watershed.
5. **60–75 s · Bookmark and reset.** Use **Demo examples** to jump to **Upper Columbia-Entiat** (17020010), then switch the map metric to **Hotspot trajectory**. Select **Reset to U.S.** to end on the national view.

## Saved examples

The dropdown selects supported cases from the installed 2030 watershed results. The current snapshot includes Lower Crab (17020015), Las Vegas Wash (15010015), Wheeler Lake (06030002), and Upper Columbia-Entiat (17020010). The readiness checker prints the current labels and identifiers.

Population describes proximity only. NASA SSP2-4.5 provides external projected climate context. Intermediate Aqueduct years are interpolated screening values. Cumulative direct water adds modeled annual demand. Permanent depletion is a separate hydrologic outcome.
