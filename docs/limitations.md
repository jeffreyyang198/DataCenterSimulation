# Limitations

- **Inventory and energy.** IM3 is a study inventory. Footprints are surface areas and serve as gross-area proxies under a specified multiplier. Verified IT floor area, measured loads and actual workloads may differ. Campus and building records may overlap. The energy model excludes campus and point records from footprint-based estimates. The climate-adjusted PUE fit uses a limited published operator sample and captures long-term differences across sites.
- **Emissions.** eGRID 2023 factors are held constant. Regional electricity-associated emissions do not locate a source plant or quantify pollution near a facility. Nearby EIA-860 generators describe available infrastructure. MW capacity and MWh generation measure different quantities.
- **Water.** Siddik coefficients are held constant. Direct demand and WF are expressed in m³, and WSF is expressed in m³-eq. WF includes direct and indirect water. Cumulative direct water is the sum of modeled annual demand and is separate from permanent depletion. Aqueduct cm/year and site m³/year have no validated common normalization here.
- **Aqueduct.** HUC8 and Aqueduct PFAF boundaries differ. Historical baseline stress is anchored at 2023 for visualization. Intermediate years interpolate from baseline to a period-centered 2030 endpoint. They are screening values rather than official annual projections.
- **Climate.** The supplied NASA CMIP6 SSP2-4.5 table contains one model. It describes external temperature context and has no feedback from projected facility consumption or emissions.
- **Population.** ACS 2019–2023 tract counts are static, and area weighting assumes uniform population within each tract. Coverage may be incomplete. Dissolution removes double counting within a group, while overlap remains between groups. Population describes proximity and cannot measure exposure or health effects.
- **Screening.** Coverage varies by metric. The scenario combinations are analytical pairings, and cluster/CRPI/hotspot labels are relative to the analyzed cohort. They support comparison and provide no causal prediction or siting determination.
